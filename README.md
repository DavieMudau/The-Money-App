# 💰 SpendWise

### Know your money. Control your spending.

**SpendWise** is a personal money-management app designed to help people understand where their money goes, plan their monthly spending, control unnecessary expenses, and work toward their savings goals.

> **Your money should have a plan.**

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://spend-wise-plan-73.lovable.app/

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Many people receive their salary or income but struggle to understand where their money goes.

Common problems include:

* Random or unnecessary spending
* Running out of money before payday
* Difficulty creating a budget
* Forgetting upcoming bills
* Struggling to save
* Not knowing how much money is safe to spend
* Having financial goals but no clear plan to reach them

SpendWise was created to make money management **simple, visual, and easy to understand.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SpendWise acts like a **calm money coach in your pocket**.

The app helps users:

**Understand → Plan → Spend → Track → Review → Save**

Instead of judging users for their spending, SpendWise provides clear information that helps them make their own financial decisions.

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 📊 Money Dashboard

Users can see their:

* Monthly income
* Total spending
* Remaining money
* Savings
* Upcoming bills
* Budget alerts

Everything is presented in one simple dashboard.

---

## 💰 Smart Budget Planner

Users enter their income and expenses, and SpendWise creates a simple spending plan based around:

* Needs
* Wants
* Savings

For example:

| Category | Suggested Allocation |
| -------- | -------------------: |
| Needs    |                  50% |
| Wants    |                  30% |
| Savings  |                  20% |

---

## 🧾 10-Second Expense Tracker

Users can quickly record an expense by entering:

* Amount
* Category
* Description

Example:

**R85 — Food — Lunch**

The remaining budget is then updated.

---

## 🛒 "Can I Afford It?"

One of SpendWise's key features.

Users enter an item and its price before purchasing it.

The app shows how that purchase could affect:

* Their remaining budget
* Their spending category
* Their savings goals

The app provides information rather than making the decision for the user.

> **Your money, your call.**

---

## 🎯 Savings Goals

Users can create financial goals such as:

* 💻 New laptop
* 🏠 Emergency fund
* 🚗 Car
* 🎓 University
* ✈️ Holiday
* 📱 New phone

Each goal includes a visual progress indicator so users can see how close they are to reaching their target.

---

## 📈 Spending Analysis

SpendWise shows users where their money went.

Example:

* Bills — 36%
* Food — 25%
* Transport — 17%
* Shopping — 11%
* Entertainment — 8%
* Other — 5%

This makes spending habits easier to understand.

---

## 🔔 Smart Spending Alerts

The app provides helpful reminders such as:

> ⚠️ You've spent 75% of your food budget.

> 💡 You have R1,200 left until payday.

> 🎯 You're R300 away from reaching your savings goal.

> 🔔 Your electricity bill is due in 3 days.

The goal is to provide **helpful guidance without guilt or judgment.**

---

## 📝 Weekly Money Review

Users can review their financial activity every week.

The review includes:

* Total money spent
* Amount saved
* Biggest spending category
* Unplanned spending
* Budget control
* Savings progress
* Bills

The app can then suggest small improvements.

# 👥 Target Users

SpendWise is designed primarily for:

* Young adults
* Students
* First-time employees
* Young professionals
* People who struggle with budgeting
* Low-to-middle-income earners
* Anyone who wants better control over their spending

The app is particularly designed for the **South African market**, using South African Rand (R) and focusing on simple everyday financial management.

# 🆚 Why SpendWise?

Traditional spreadsheets require users to maintain everything manually.

Calculators only answer individual questions.

Many budgeting tools can feel complicated or assume users already understand personal finance.

SpendWise focuses on one simple everyday question:

> **"Can I afford this without hurting my financial goals?"**

The app provides simple information at the moment the user needs it.
